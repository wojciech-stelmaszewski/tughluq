# Plan — align remaining match rules (2026-09-16_06-06)

Status: implemented.

## What already agrees

20 rounds, 64 = 4×16, 7-card opening with specials inside, own-suit sums, shotgun anytime / waste on human / one-use, GAME OVER on no number cards or shotgun kill, majority at the end, winner takes one table card, vaccine not on self.

Face values (A=14) and ties stay guesses — sources never state them.

## What does not agree

1. Pairing is in-group only. On screen you choose anyone, then touch. Random legal play pairs any two living players.
2. Vaccine cures any infected opponent. JA: it cancels a **Zombie card placed this duel**, then that player is human again. After a cancel, the number sums decide. A vaccine with no Z on the table is spent and does nothing.
3. Playing Z removes it from the attacker. JA: the opponent **also** receives a copy. The attacker keeps Z and can infect again.
4. A play dumps every card of the chosen suit. Sources only say “play same-suit cards and sum them”. Random legal play picks a non-empty subset.

## Out of scope

Shotgun vs played Z (show later, not quoted). Exact vaccine count. Operator-locked own-suit and 7-card hands.
