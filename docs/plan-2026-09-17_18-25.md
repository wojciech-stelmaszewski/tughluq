# Plan — spend a real training budget before adding capacity (2026-09-17_18-25)

Status: proposed.

## Need

[train-log.md](./train-log.md) concludes that the linear scorer found the “play Zombie”
neighbourhood but did not dominate `aggressive`. That conclusion is not supported by the run
behind it.

The run used the CLI defaults: 8 generations × 8 candidates × 5 episodes. With the probe
episodes that is **416 episodes**. The engine does ~320 episodes per second, so the search
that we are calling a plateau consumed **about 1.3 seconds of compute**.

Two separate problems, both cheap to fix.

## Problem 1 — the monitoring columns are not measurements

`src/rl/train.ts:111` caps the probe at four episodes:

    const probeEpisodes = Math.max(1, Math.min(options.episodes, 4));

Every per-generation column in the log — vs `randomLegal`, vs `aggressive`, zombie share — is
therefore computed on ≤ 4 matches. That is why zombie share reads 0.500, 0.750 and 1.000: those
are 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4, not population statistics. The only trustworthy numbers in the file are
the 50-episode held-out rows at the bottom.

Fix: probe episodes become their own parameter, independent of the per-candidate budget, and
default high enough to be read as a trend.

## Problem 2 — the search budget is nominal

Selection quality depends on the per-candidate episode count. Stage A measured a per-episode
differential spread of roughly 0.14, so five episodes give a standard error near **±0.063** —
the same order as the differences between candidates being ranked.

`trainEs` already evaluates every candidate of a generation on the same `genSeed`
(`src/rl/train.ts:86`), so the comparison is paired and much of that noise cancels. Common
random numbers make small budgets usable; they do not make eight mutations over eight
generations a search.

Fix: no algorithm change. Raise generations, population and episodes, and record what happens.

## What we do **not** change

- No MLP. Adding capacity before spending the budget would treat a sampling problem as a
  representation problem.
- No PPO, no reward shaping, no new features, no sigma schedule.
- The ES stays a (1+λ) hill climber with an elite and a promoting reference league.

## Budget

60 generations × 24 candidates × 50 episodes, probe 200.

    60 × (24 × 50 + 3 × 200) = 108,000 episodes ≈ 6 minutes

## Success

Either outcome is a result worth recording:

1. The elite beats `aggressive` head-to-head (today: −0.070) on a 200-episode held-out check,
   and Stage C becomes optional.
2. It still does not, on a budget large enough that the word “plateau” is earned. Then the MLP
   in task 10 has a reason to exist.

## Review against the core principles

**KISS.** Three new CLI numbers and one default change. No new concepts.

**YAGNI.** `--probe`, `--sigma` and `--promote` are added because a budget sweep cannot be run
without them. Nothing else.

**SRP.** `trainEs` keeps owning the search; the CLI keeps owning argument parsing and reporting.

**DRY.** Reuses `evaluateDifferential` and the existing probe path unchanged.
