# Watch match loop — 20 random-legal rounds

**Session start:** 2026-09-15_11-02  
**Status:** implemented  

## Goal

`/watch` becomes a stepped match, not a static deal. The 3D lab stays on `/` with **no in-app links** either way.

## Play

- One click plays **one** of 20 rounds.
- Pair living players **inside each group** (bye if odd).
- Each table draws a suit. Follow that suit with a number card if able; Shotgun / Zombie / Vaccine stay legal when the rules allow.
- Policy this slice: **uniform random among legal cards**.
- After 20 rounds (or when no pair remains), majority of living humans vs zombies.

Working rulings (until the show text is sharper):

- One card each; higher follow-suit rank wins. Ace high (14).
- Shotgun resolves first: kills an infected opponent; wasted on a human.
- Vaccine is legal only vs an infected opponent; it cures them (status + remove Z cards). Not on self.
- Playing Zombie keeps the striker infected, infects the loser, then adds a Z card to the loser **after** the winner steals one card.
- Zero number cards → eliminated at end of the round.

## Report

A chronological log under the table: every duel in every finished round (names, suit, cards, outcome).
