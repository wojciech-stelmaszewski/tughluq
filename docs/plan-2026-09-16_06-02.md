# Plan — lock two opening rules (2026-09-16_06-02)

Status: implemented.

## Locks (operator)

1. In a mini-game each player **chooses one suit from their own hand** and plays those number cards. Compare **sums**. The two piles do not have to match.
2. Opening hand is **exactly 7 cards, specials included**. Shotgun / Zombie / Vaccine occupy slots.

## Why

The audit left both items open. The operator closed them. The engine already does (1). The deal still deals 7 numbers **plus** extras.

## Change

- `HAND_SIZE = 7`. Assign shotgun (always), one zombie, then vaccines. Fill the remaining slots with number cards so every opening hand has length 7.
- Typical human: 6 numbers + shotgun. Seed zombie: 5 + shotgun + Z. Vaccine holder: 5 + shotgun + V. Both: 4 + shotgun + Z + V.
- Infection during play may still **add** a Zombie card (canon copy). That is after the opening deal.
- Own-suit + sum: keep `legalPlays` as-is. Update docs so this is no longer marked as a guess.
- 3D deal animation: walk the 7 slots in hand order, not “7 numbers then extras”.

## Out of scope

Pairing-by-touch, vaccine-only-on-played-Z, whether the attacker keeps Z, face-card values, subset vs full suit.

## Review

KISS: one size constant, specials first, then fill. YAGNI: no new deal parameters. SRP: still `deal()`.
