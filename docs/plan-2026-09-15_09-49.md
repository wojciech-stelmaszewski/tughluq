# Visual polish — rounded cards, private tables, lab lighting

**Session start:** 2026-09-15_09-49  
**Status:** implemented  
**Scope:** scene presentation only. Deal engine unchanged.

## Goal

Three graphic notes from playtesting:

1. Playing-card **corners must be rounded** (3D mesh and HUD thumbs).
2. **Each player sits at their own table**, not along one communal bench.
3. The wing **materials and lighting** must look finished — a sterile research hall, not unlit white boxes.

## Constraints

- Keep Phase 1 rules and the deal engine as they are.
- Stay on the National Institute of Virus Research look: white, quiet, architectural. No neon casino.
- KISS: one extruded card geometry, one station prefab, lighting from drei helpers (Environment / Lightformer / MeshReflectorMaterial). No custom GLSL unless a stock material cannot do the job.
- YAGNI: no character meshes, no walkthrough, no extra wings.

## Layout

- Split the group into two rows facing a central aisle (same north/south split as now).
- Each seat owns a small lab table + stool. Cards land on that table.
- Dealer stack and the metallic elimination dome sit on a central podium. Deal animation still flies from there.
- Camera pulls back so a 16-player wing reads as a room, not a single object.

## Cards

`BoxGeometry` cannot round the silhouette: a 8 mm thick box caps corner radius at ~4 mm. Use a shared `ExtrudeGeometry` of a rounded rectangle (face / back / edge material groups). HUD thumbs use a playing-card `border-radius`.

## Room

Replace the cheap emissive ceiling tiles and floor ring with:

- Reflective epoxy floor (`MeshReflectorMaterial`)
- Soft studio fill (`Environment` + `Lightformer`)
- Recessed linear ceiling LEDs, satin walls, a wing arch, cove light
- One key directional with shadows + contact shadows

## Out of scope

Gameplay, pairing tables for later duels, scanned Netflix set dressing.
